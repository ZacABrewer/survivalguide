Most survival deaths come from a short list of mistakes: eating the wrong plant, drinking bad water, getting cold and wet, carbon monoxide, getting lost and moving the wrong way. These rules guard against all of them. Learn them before anything else in this guide.

The Golden Rules

  1. Call for help first. If you can reach emergency services, do it before anything else: 911 in the US and Canada, 112 across the European Union and in many other countries, 999 in the UK. For a possible poisoning in the US, call Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222.
  2. Stop, think, observe, plan. Panic kills more people than a lack of gear does. Sit down, breathe, take stock, then act.
  3. Never eat a wild plant or mushroom unless you are 100% sure what it is. Use several features, not one, and more than one source. Wild mushrooms need confirmation from an expert in person, every time.
  4. Learn the look-alikes before the edible. Know the deadly plant that resembles your food before you pick the food.
  5. Treat all wild water as unsafe until you purify it, no matter how clear, cold or fast-flowing it looks.
  6. Never run a fire, stove, heater, grill or generator inside a tent, snow shelter, vehicle, garage or closed room. Carbon monoxide has no smell and kills in your sleep.
  7. Stay dry, and stay off the cold ground. Wet clothing and bare ground pull heat from your body fast. Insulate underneath you as well as around you.
  8. Tell someone where you're going, and stay put if you're lost. Rescuers search the route you left behind. Wandering makes you harder to find.
  9. Follow tested methods for food safety. Canning, curing and fermenting go wrong silently. Botulism has no smell or taste.
  10. Treat herbs like medicine. They can interact with prescription drugs and can harm a pregnancy, a child or a sick person.
  11. Training beats reading. Take a first-aid and CPR course, and practice skills like fire and shelter before you need them.
  12. When in doubt, choose the cautious option. Hungry is better than poisoned. A wasted rescue call is better than a death.

Warning

Reading about a skill is not the same as being able to do it. Practice first in safe conditions with someone experienced.

What This Guide Leaves Out on Purpose

  • Making explosives or incendiaries, including black powder and thermite. Blacksmithing in this guide joins metal by forge welding instead.
  • Making firearms or weapons beyond traditional hunting and fishing tools.
  • Making or extracting drugs, and doses or preparation of psychoactive substances. Psychoactive plants are covered for recognition, risks and first aid only.
  • Anything meant to harm people.
