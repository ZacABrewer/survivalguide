## Disagreements
- Homemade ORS: 6 level tsp sugar + 1/2 level tsp salt per liter (Rehydration Project home page, WikEM citing WHO cholera task-force paper) versus 8 tsp sugar + 1 tsp salt (older Rehydration Project packaged page). Chapter uses the weaker 6 + 1/2 and says so. The WHO fact sheet and CDC pages could not be opened, so the "state the source" requirement is met via secondary hosts (Rehydration Project / WikEM), not WHO or CDC directly.
- Chemical eye flush time: 15 minutes minimum (Prevent Blindness) vs 15 to 20 (Better Health). Chapter says aim for 20.
- Tooth rinse: Mayo says milk or saliva and not tap water; Smiles for Life says saline or milk. Chapter lists milk or saline, avoid tap water.
- Fast-breathing limits: MSF says 1 to 11 months = 50+, under 1 month = 60+; the IMCI-based module says 2 months to under 12 months = 50+. Chapter shows MSF limits.

## Could NOT verify (left out or flagged in the chapter)
