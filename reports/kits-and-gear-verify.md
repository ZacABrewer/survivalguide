# Verify report: kits-and-gear (Part 1 Ch 3 "Kits & Gear")

Verifier: independent fact-check, November 16, 2026. File: /home/claude/survival-site/content/kits-and-gear.html

## Changes made
1. Winter stranding steps: engine run time changed from "5 to 10 min/hour (Michigan only)" to "no more than about 10 min/hour", citing Michigan State Police (5-10), NWS North Platte (~10) and Maine MEMA (~10); use shorter time if fuel low. Tailpipe check now required before every engine start, and "never run the engine if you cannot clear it" (Maine MEMA: only run if sure exhaust is clear). Added seat-belt step (Michigan), limb movement and heart-strain warning (NWS North Platte). Added NWS and Maine MEMA to Sources.
2. CO callout (stuck car): added "call 911".
3. Stockpile intro: "Red Cross and CDC both say keep at least 2 weeks" corrected; Red Cross says 2 weeks at home, CDC says 3 days minimum and 2 weeks if possible.
4. Food: added conservative planning rule (rotate cans within about a year, UGA best-quality guidance; NDSU allows 18 mo high-acid / 2-5 yr low-acid). Bad-can callout now includes sharp dents (esp. seams), spurting liquid, foul odor (NDSU FN1468), and "never taste to test" (CDC).
5. Power outage: added CDC "discard food with unusual color, odor or texture".
6. Fuel storage: removed unverified "Lehi also advises far less than the maximum". Added US Army safety guidance (army.mil): no gasoline in living space or vehicles, detached shed/garage safest, keep minimum, fill cans on the ground, leave expansion room. Army article added to Sources.
7. Generator callout: "CO can reach deadly levels in minutes" now attributed to CPSC (alert says it "can kill you in minutes"); CO alarms: battery or battery-backup, CPSC test monthly, CDC check batteries at clock change and replace per maker or every 5 years. Maintenance table row updated to match.
8. Sleeping bags: extreme rating now noted as based on a standard woman, with "never choose a bag by this number"; added "if you sleep cold, apply the buffer to the comfort rating" and REI's note that ratings assume a pad of about R 4.
9. Maintenance: "Red Cross says to check kits often and replace used or expired items" was not on the cited Red Cross kit page; replaced with Red Cross winter-storm advice to check and restock the vehicle kit.
10. Figure 7: heat-flow arrow pointed UP from the ground (wrong direction); reversed to point down, label changed to "Body heat flows into ground". Rating ladder labels clarified ("highest °F", "lower °F", "survival only, lowest"). Re-rendered and checked.

## Claims verified (pages opened)
- CDC water page: 1 gal/person/day, 3 days min, 2 weeks if possible; food-grade containers; never bleach/pesticide containers; 1 tsp unscented bleach per quart sanitize; 50-70°F; away from gasoline; replace every 6 months; label "drinking water" + date. All match.
- Red Cross Survival Kit Supplies: 1 gal/day, 3-day evacuation, 2-week home, 7-day meds, kit item list (cash, documents, radio, multi-tool, maps, blanket). Match.
- Red Cross Winter Storm: avoid candles (fire risk), generators outdoors, car-kit items, check/restock kit. Match.
- CPSC Portable Generator Safety Alert: 20 ft from doors/windows/vents; never in home, garage, basement, crawlspace, shed even with ventilation; cool before refuel; labeled non-glass containers outside living areas, away from fuel-burning appliances; outdoor heavy-duty cords rated above load; no backfeeding; CO alarms tested monthly. Match.
- CDC CO page: generator >20 ft, never in home/garage even with doors open; detectors near sleeping areas, batteries at clock change, replace per maker or 5 yr; no camp stove/charcoal indoors; never run car in attached garage; symptoms headache, dizziness, weakness. Match.
- CDC power outage: fridge 4 h, full freezer 48 h, half 24 h; discard listed perishables after 4 h. Match.
- NDSU FN1468 and UGA Extension shelf lives as tabled. Match; disagreements disclosed.
- Michigan State Police, NWS North Platte, Maine MEMA stranded-car guidance. Match after edits.
- NWS Houston and NHTSA: ~20°F rise in 10 min, 120°F within minutes, cracked windows little help. Match.
- Lehi City fuel limits (25 gal gasoline, 10 gal attached garage, none in basement, two 1-lb propane cylinders indoors, 2012 IFC). Match.
- US Forest Service "An Ax to Grind": felling axe head 3-6 lb, handle 30-36 in; hatchet; double-bit edges; splitting axe. Match.
- REI sleeping bag, sleeping pads, pad blog: 10-15°F buffer, R-value table, R-values additive, foam under air pad, F3340 adopted 2020, ratings assume ~R4 pad. Match.
- Australian Hiker (secondary): ISO 23537 comfort (standard woman, relaxed), lower limit (standard man, curled, 8 h), extreme (survival only, hypothermia risk), heated manikin on mat. Match.
- Cross-references: all names on spec list, plus "Part 1 › Planning" and "Part 1 › Clothing & Layering" (accepted by main session). CO first aid cross-ref to Part 7 › Bites, Stings & Poisoning confirmed (that chapter has a Carbon Monoxide section). Part 7 › Medical Kits & Medicines four tiers confirmed.
