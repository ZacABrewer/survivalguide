## Claims verified (re-fetched)
- US Forest Service "If You Get Lost": STOP = stop, think, observe, plan; panic the greatest enemy; stay put at nightfall, injured, or near exhaustion; stream-downhill only a last resort, often difficult and dangerous. Matches. (Leaving a note is not on that page; it is generic advice, kept.)
- CDC "When You're Sick": back to normal activities after 24 hours with symptoms improving and no fever without fever-reducing medicine, then extra precautions for 5 days; trouble breathing/chest pain = emergency care. Matches.
- WHO Jan 2020 home-care guidance: well-ventilated single room; open windows in shared spaces; one healthy caregiver with no risk conditions; no visitors; 1 m distance; tight medical mask, change when wet/soiled; bleach 1:99 of ~5% hypochlorite; daily cleaning; laundry soap or 60–90°C machine wash; bag linen without shaking; lined bin in room. Matches.
- AMSEA Life Raft Survival: one flare early, save the rest; EPIRB on and outside; sea anchor slows drift; seasickness meds early; hydrate from the start including first 24 h (some manuals wrongly say otherwise); set daily ration; do not eat without water; lookout; stay tied to vessel while safe. Matches.
- Ready.gov Power Outages: 40°F for 2 hours or more → discard; refrigerated medicine rule; unplug appliances; never heat with gas stove/oven; generators outdoors. Matches.
