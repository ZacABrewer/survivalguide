## Could not verify / sources disagree
- Tourniquet time noting: stopthebleed.org, ACS and Red Cross pages did not state it. Only the military TCCC card instructions confirm recording type and time. Card says "write the time and tell responders."
- S.T.O.P.: the US Forest Service page confirms Stop/Think/Observe/Plan. The Rule of 3s numbers come from UW Extension (Army ATP 3-50.21 text did not state them). The rule is flagged as rough.
- Kindling sizes: no authoritative source gave pencil/thumb/wrist measurements, so none were given.
- Boiling altitude: CDC says 3 min above 6,500 ft; EPA says above 5,000 ft. Page uses the more conservative 5,000 ft.
- Bleach: EPA table lists only 6% and 8.25% (no 5 gallon row); CDC gives 5-9% = 8 drops/gal and 5 gal = 40 drops. EPA 8.25% says 6 drops/gal, CDC says 8. Page shows all and advises 8 if unsure.
- Snakebite limb position: WMS says heart level, CDC disaster page says below heart, CDC/NIOSH says neutral. Page states the disagreement. Pressure immobilization for non-US snakes rests on NOLS only.
- Hypothermia wet clothing: Red Cross/CDC say remove; WMS summary (via AFP) says keep on until warm. Page blends and flags it.
- Heat stroke fans: Red Cross endorses fanning; NWS warns at high heat index. Flagged. Immersion duration: Red Cross 15 min; BC source gives 2-15C (35-60F) water; page says cool at least 20 minutes (Red Cross alternative methods).
- Choking: Red Cross and AHA 2025 agree (5 back blows, 5 abdominal thrusts). Older AHA guidance differed.
- AHA hands-only fact sheet gave only rate; depth (2 in) and hand position came from the Heartsaver 2025 summary hosted by VUMC and the Red Cross page.
- Ground symbols: FAA AIM 6-2-6 text via faraim.org mirror (not faa.gov) confirms V, X, N, Y and 10 ft minimum. The arrow is "proceeding in this direction"; the mirror text did not describe its shape. Colorado Parks and Wildlife says arrows mean direction.
- Three-fire spacing of 30-50 ft and "keep one burning" rest on a single source (Colorado Parks and Wildlife).
- Not verified, so omitted: Android satellite SOS, iodine doses beyond a brief note, Canada text details beyond the CRTC page (Nunavut has no 9-1-1 per CRTC). Frostbite ibuprofen dose omitted.
- Text-to-911 message content advice not verified (FCC page silent), so omitted.
