## Not verified / blocked / sources disagree
- WHO snakebite management guidelines (2005 SEARO and 2016 2nd ed. PDFs): fetch was blocked by the tool ("PROVENANCE_REQUIRED"). Not retried. So WHO first-aid wording is taken from the WHO fact sheet plus the 2018 PMC review (which cites WHO) and NOLS/CDC. I could not verify a WHO rule on pressure immobilization for kraits, cobras, or mambas. The text says so and gives conservative advice.
- WMS snakebite practice guideline: original not opened (wmsdev PDF robots-blocked; no PubMed abstract found). NOLS (WMS-based) and CDC/NIOSH used instead.
- ANZCOR guideline pages not reachable (failed fetch). Used University of Melbourne AVRU, NSW Poisons Information, MJA, and Australian Museum for pressure immobilization. A survive-student-resource.pages.dev mirror of ANZCOR appeared in search; I did not use it.
- Sling disagreement: NSW says splint and sling for hand/forearm bites; Melbourne says do not use a bent-elbow sling. The text takes the conservative line.
- Limb position: NOLS/MedlinePlus say heart level, CDC says neutral, PMC review says individualize. Text uses heart level.
- Washing: NOLS says wash in North America, not in Australia, no massage in India. CDC/NIOSH says wash. Both given.
- Box jellyfish pain: Australian Prescriber lists ice packs; AAFP review says heat beats cold. Text notes the split.
- Amatoxin onset: CMAJ 6-24 h, Missouri 6-12 h. Text uses 6-24 h.
- Brazilian wandering spider (Phoneutria): no authoritative source opened. Text gives only generic care and says so.
- Cone snail first aid outside Australia and regional tick diseases outside the US and CCHF: not verified; flagged in text. Tick-borne encephalitis and non-US Lyme not covered.
- Tents/CO: CDC page does not name tents; the text says this is plain logic. No source for a specific tent rule.
- Poison Help US number: poison.org says the National Capital Poison Center closed its call center on March 31, 2025 and is digital only. MedlinePlus and Poison Control still list 1-800-222-1222. The text states both. Check before release.
- Not tied to a fetched source: "shake out boots" tips; "do not handle live cone shells"; "check fingertips and loosen if pale/cold/blue" (labeled as my reading); "send two people for help" (general practice).
