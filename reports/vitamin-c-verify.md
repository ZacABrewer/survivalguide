## Verified (brief)

- NIH ODS: every RDA, AI, pregnancy, and lactation value; smokers +35 mg; ULs 400/650/1,200/1,800/2,000, none set for infants; scurvy within about 1 month at <10 mg/day; early and later signs; untreated scurvy is fatal; GI side effects; kidney-stone and hemochromatosis caveats; steaming or microwaving limits losses. All 11 NIH food-table values match exactly.
- StatPearls: onset 4–12 weeks; treatment 500–1,000 mg/day for 1–3 months; recovery at 24 h, 1–2 weeks, about 1 month, and about 3 months; surgery for severe bone disease; risk groups; severe signs (anasarca, jaundice, seizures, organ failure, death). Both onset timelines are attributed in the text.
- USDA: rose hips 426 mg/100 g (FDC 168998) and raw beef liver 1.3 mg/100 g (FDC 169451), confirmed via a site that mirrors SR Legacy. FDC itself and its API were unreachable through WebFetch.
- Bar chart: every bar is exactly 0.75 px/mg (e.g., 426 → 319.5, 1.3 → 0.975), and the 90 mg line is at 317.5. The potato chart is 4 px per percent and matches the PubMed abstract (Mareschi 1983: 50% at 3 months, 66% at 6–8 months, 40–45% peeled, 7% unpeeled, up to 70% held hot).
- Cooking-methods abstract: 0–91.1% retention; microwave best, boiling worst. FAO AGRIS dried vegetables: vitamin C gone in some onions and carrots by 12 months. K-State MF1181: 50–70°F, use within a year, avoid >95°F.
- Lind 1747: HMS Salisbury, 12 men, one citrus patient fit for duty at about 6 days, the other nursed the rest (WHO Bulletin, James Lind Library).
- Yew: NC State rates poison severity high; taxines; bark, leaves, seeds, and stems poisonous; flat needles in two rows; red cup-shaped aril. The yew-needle tea poisoning statement is confirmed (NCPC). Ponderosa: green or dry needles cause abortion in cattle; isocupressic acid also in lodgepole, common juniper, and Monterey cypress; the page says nothing about humans (USDA ARS). Norfolk Island pine "not a true pine" (Purdue). Ho-Chunk tree lists and tea method match the steps.
- Sprouting (UNL): bleach ¾ cup per gallon for 5 min, ½–1 inch of seed, rinse 2–4 times a day, 68–72°F, 3–6 days, sunny window for a few hours. UConn: 40°F, organic seed no safer, discard moldy or slimy batches. VT: no seeds from poisonous plants or old or moldy seeds; planting seed may be treated.
- Poison Control 1-800-222-1222 (confirmed on poison.org). 90 mg × 365 = 32.85 g, about 33 g, a little over 1 oz.

An estimate puts all SVG text inside the viewBoxes. Copyright: no copied sentences, and the only quotation is the generic word "glue".
