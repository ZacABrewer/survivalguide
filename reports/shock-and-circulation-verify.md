## Claims verified
- Red Cross shock: signs; lie flat, feet up about 6–12 in only without injury; other position if it eases breathing; nothing to eat or drink; warm/cool; recovery position if vomiting or less alert. AHA/ARC 2024 agrees (supine; leg raise 6–12 in, Class 2b, no trauma; return to flat if discomfort).
- NOLS shock: treat until ruled out; legs up is debatable but harmless; evacuate if vitals don't stabilize.
- Red Cross abdominal injury: all evisceration steps exactly match (do not push back or press; moist dressing with clean warm tap water or saline; loose plastic wrap/foil; on back, knees bent unless painful; leave as found if spine injury possible).
- TCCC: vented seal first, non-vented if none, monitor, burp/remove; tension pneumothorax signs; position supine/recovery unless conscious and needs to sit up.
- Merck rib fractures: cough/deep breathe about hourly; flail segment moves opposite; ventilator/surgery.
- Red Cross heart attack: rest in comfortable position, loosen clothing, help with prescribed nitroglycerin, CPR/AED readiness.
- CDC stroke: BE-FAST; don't drive, call 911; note time; TIA needs care.
