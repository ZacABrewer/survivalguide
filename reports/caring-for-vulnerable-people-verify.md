## Claims verified (re-fetched)
- CDC Powdered Formula in an Emergency: soap and water or ≥60% alcohol sanitizer; bottled water until tap is declared safe; water first then powder, exact amounts, shake; warm in a bowl of warm water, wrist test, never microwave; use within 1 hour of starting a feed and 2 hours of preparation; fresh formula each feed in an outage; discard dented/rusted/puffy/broken-seal or flood-exposed cans; disposable cups when bottles cannot be cleaned; ready-to-feed especially for <2 months, premature, weak immune systems. Matches.
- CDC Special Considerations: discard opened/prepared formula after 4 hours without power or above 40°F; expressed milk in cooler with frozen packs up to 24 h; milk with ice crystals can be refrozen; fully thawed milk used within 24 h, not refrozen; throw away nipples/pacifiers that touched flood water. Matches.
- FDA insulin: 59–86°F (15–30°C) up to 28 days for opened or unopened manufacturer vials/cartridges; normal 36–46°F; do not use frozen insulin; above 86°F less effective the longer exposed. Matches. Consistent with Part 7 › Medical Kits & Medicines (which says "limited time"; this chapter gives the FDA's specific 28 days and says to check the label).
- Alzheimer's Association: ~60% wander; call 911 if not found within 15 minutes; most found within half a mile; helpline 800-272-3900; stay calm; tell shelter/hotel staff. Matches.
