- Text-to-911: FCC pages give no roaming, location, or length limits. Chapter says only what FCC states (availability varies, voice preferred, bounce-back).
- Satellite messengers "most need a paid plan": taken from the existing signaling-rescue chapter (REI), not re-opened by me.
- Heat stroke, frostbite thawing, and tourniquet no-loosen rule rely on Red Pages (not re-verified by me). Heat-stroke cooling times deliberately not repeated.
- CPR: no single WMS rule for remote termination found. Cited drowning (30 min / 90 min cold / 25 min CPR), avalanche (30 min), hypothermia (check pulse a full minute; 5 min on / up to 5 off when moving), and the NAEMSP wilderness EMS 30-minute protocol (exceptions: hypothermia, lightning, cold-water drowning; its own source is unattributed). Lightning specifics and traumatic-arrest rules NOT verified (NWS page lacked them; WMS lightning PDF blocked by robots), so left out.
- Tourniquet conversion: only described the rule "do not convert without training" (TCCC proposal: lay reassess within 2 h, conversion after 2 h for medical staff; Australian Army: <2 h safe, 2-6 h risky without monitoring, >6 h unwarranted). TCCC item is a proposed change, only the abstract was read.
- Anaphylaxis with no epinephrine: Red Cross page does not say; no verified substitute found. Stated as such.
- Snakebite: CDC NIOSH list used; pressure-immobilization not covered (unverified).
