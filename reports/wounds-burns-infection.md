## Not verified / weak
- WMS 2025 Burns guideline: original NOT opened (paywalled/not found). Cooling 20-30 min at 12-18 C, fluid thresholds, evacuation thresholds, honey/ointment, "palm overestimates" come from a blog summary and a course slide deck. Flagged in text.
- WMS wound guideline 2014 is the newest wound guideline found. It contradicts itself on psi (6-12 vs 6-15) and on closure windows (6/10 h vs 8-12/24 h). Text uses the shorter windows.
- Burn cooling time: Red Cross 5-20, MedlinePlus 5-30, AAD ~10, WMS 2025 (secondary) 20-30. Guide uses about 20 min.
- Petroleum jelly on minor burns: AAD allows after cooling, Red Cross says no greasy products. Guide takes the cautious line.
- Fishhook string-yank: backed only by AAFP 2001 and an EMRA emergency-medicine article. No wilderness-medicine source found. Flagged in text.
- Tourniquet duration: no verified safe maximum for lay rescuers. Used TCCC 2016 (convert within 2 h if conditions met; do not remove after 6 h) and course slides. TCCC text is from 2016. The tourniquet-conversion paragraph "watch for bleeding to return" is a mild inference from the TCCC criteria.
- Dressing change frequency: WMS gives none. Guide says change when wet/dirty and check daily, labeled as the guide's own rule.
- Amputated part: no time limit verified.
- Impaled-object exceptions beyond OHSU slides (e.g., airway, CPR) not verified; left out.
- Not tied to a fetched source: "make sure power is off before touching an electrical victim"; define lymphangitis as spread along lymph channels (Red Cross/MedlinePlus only mention red streaks); splinter removal with clean tweezers; ORS recipe only from OHSU slides.
- Failed fetches: American Burn Association, Red Cross cuts/amputation/impaled/chemical pages, CDC rabies clinical page, AAD blisters. Abscess home care (warm compress) not verified, so omitted.
