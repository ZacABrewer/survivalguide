## Not verified / disagreements
- Search budget: 7 of 8 WebSearch calls used. The CDC and Wayback copies of "Caring for Someone Sick at Home" would not load, so pandemic home-care steps rest on the WHO January 2020 interim guidance plus the current CDC "when sick" page (which gives few room, mask, or cleaning details). The WHO guidance is for one virus; its isolation length (two negative tests, 14-day contact monitoring) was left out and the CDC return rule used instead. Flagged in the text.
- WHO says keep 1 m from the patient; that distance is used as written. No source checked gave a mask-type rule for caregivers beyond "medical mask, snug fit".
- Life raft: AMSEA is a secondary source. Its water figure (1.5 L per person in SOLAS A packs) was left out. A ration amount is not given; the Army "set a daily ration" rule is used. AMSEA's "drink from the start" differs from some older manuals, and the text says so.
- Winter-car engine run time differs (Michigan State Police 5 to 10 min; NWS/Maine about 10); both are shown, shorter if fuel is low. Carried over from kits-and-gear.
- Lost-person advice on following a stream downhill: Forest Service lists it as a last resort; NPS (via when-help-isnt-coming) calls creek-following dangerous. Both are stated; the conservative advice is to stay.
- No source opened gave a rescue timeline or survival odds, so the "first week" rows give routines, not predictions.
- Whistle signal counts and pandemic mask grades were not verified and are left to Part 9 › Signaling, Rescue & Communication.
- Ready.gov page URL has the odd "/ar/node/11963" form (it was in search results and fetched fine); the main ready.gov/power-outages URL failed to fetch.
- Verified date line uses November 16, 2026, matching sibling chapters.
