## Claims verified
- **Tourniquet** (Red Cross, USUHS JIT, DoD FAQ, JTS DD1380):
  - 2-3 in above the wound, between the wound and the heart, not on a joint or the wound.
  - Tighten until bleeding stops; it hurts.
  - Second tourniquet goes above the first.
  - Never loosen or remove it.
  - Record the type and time (JTS).
- **Bleeding**: half a soda can is serious; layer dressings without removing the first; pack junctional wounds; do not remove embedded objects (Red Cross).
- **CPR** (AHA 2025 BLS, Heartsaver 2025, Red Cross):
  - Rate 100-120/min.
  - Depth at least 2 in (5 cm), at most 2.4 in (6 cm).
  - Hands on the lower half of the sternum, full recoil, pauses under 10 s, 30:2.
  - AED pads upper right chest and lower left side; resume compressions after the shock.
  - Hands-only for teens and adults (AHA fact sheet).
- **Choking**: back blows first, then abdominal thrusts, both 5 each, in alternating cycles. Chest thrusts for pregnant or large people. Look in the mouth and remove an object only if you see it (AHA 2025, Red Cross).
- **Shock**: signs; 6-12 in leg raise only with no trauma; recovery position if airway risk; nothing by mouth (Red Cross).
- **Hypothermia**: 60-second pulse check, horizontal position, gentle handling, drinks only if alert, no alcohol or caffeine (WMS/AFP, Red Cross, CDC, MedlinePlus).
- **Frostbite**: 37-39°C (98.6-102.2°F), about 30 min, endpoint soft and red/purple, no rubbing, no dry heat, no walking on frozen feet, elevate, ibuprofen (WMS/AFP, CDC).
- **Heat**: NWS 103°F threshold and 1-hour rule; fans unhelpful above the high 90s; NIOSH cooling sites; Red Cross immersion; no salt tablets.
- **Snakebite**: do not drive yourself; remove rings; wash; mark the swelling with the time; the full do-not list (NIOSH, CDC, WMS 2015).
- **Stings**: stinger removal, wrapped cold pack, epinephrine technique (outer thigh, 90°, 3 s, rub 10 s), second dose at 5-10 min (Red Cross); spider care (NIOSH); scorpion red flags (Red Cross).
- **Poison**: 1-800-222-1222 (free, 24/7, US only), skin rinse 15 min, eye rinse 15-20 min with room-temperature water, no induced vomiting (poison.org, MedlinePlus, Red Cross); CO actions (EPA); delayed mushroom symptoms (poison.org).
- **Water** (EPA, CDC):
  - EPA 6%: 2 drops per quart, 8 per gallon, 16 per 2 gal (1/4 tsp).
  - EPA 8.25%: 2 drops per quart, 6 per gallon, 12 per 2 gal (1/8 tsp).
  - CDC 5-9%: 2, 8, and 40 drops per 5 gal (1/2 tsp).
  - Double for cloudy, colored, or very cold water. Wait 30 min. Re-dose if there is no chlorine smell, then wait 15 min.
  - Boil 1 min; 3 min above 5,000 ft (EPA) or above 6,500 ft (CDC).
  - Bleach and iodine are weak against parasites.
- **Calls**: 112 in the EU (Your Europe); 999/112 in the UK (GOV.UK); text-to-911 limited, with a bounce-back if unavailable (FCC, 911.gov).
- **Beacons and satellite**: PLB is manual-only, 406 MHz, registration required, 24 h minimum battery (NOAA SARSAT). iPhone 14 or later satellite SOS needs a clear view of the sky and horizon (Apple).
- **Ground-to-air code** (FAA AIM 6-2-6 figure on faa.gov):
  - V = require assistance; X = require medical assistance; N = no; Y = yes; arrow = proceeding in this direction.
  - Symbols at least 10 ft; maximum contrast; if in doubt, SOS.
  - On snow, depressed areas appear black from the air.
  - Pilots acknowledge by rocking the wings.
- **S.T.O.P.** (USFS). **Rule of 3s** (UW Extension). **Signal fires**: 30-50 ft triangle, apex fire kept burning (CPW).
- **Fire**: reflector wall, wet wood dry inside, 10x fuel, no gasoline, drown and stir (CPW, Navy, NPS).
