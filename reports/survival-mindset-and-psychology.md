## Could not verify / sources disagree / judgment calls
- **OODA loop:** could not open a source, so it is not mentioned by name. The chapter uses a plain four-step "Look, Choose, Act, Check" loop, labeled as this guide's own design.
- **Heart-rate thresholds for tunnel vision / fine motor loss:** the only source giving bpm numbers (Lewinski, Police Chief) cites no studies. Marlatte 2025 sets no threshold and did not measure tunnel vision or fine motor skill directly. The chapter drops all numbers. Tunnel vision is described only as reported by Hill and as an authors' interpretation in Marlatte.
- **Laurence Gonzales (synthesis) and Red Cross / APA / SAMHSA / NCTSN primary pages:** not opened; not cited. NCTSN/NCPTSD content is cited only through the hosted PFA parent tips file.
- **Box breathing "count 4":** Balban used 3 to 4 s (shortest tier) up to 5 to 10 s per phase, set by a breath test. "Start at 4, shorten if straining, stop if dizzy" is this guide's judgment. The "stop if dizzy" safety note is also this guide's judgment.
- **Panic-attack chest-pain warning** (do not assume panic; treat chest pain as a possible heart attack) is this guide's conservative judgment, not from a fetched source. The Mental Health Foundation page itself says to remind yourself it is not a heart attack.
- **Panic response steps (Fig 4) and morale routine (Fig 6), roles chart (Fig 7), decision checklist, "count small wins," slipping-morale signs, behavioral signs of panic, confusion-in-elders-may-be-physical:** this guide's own designs or judgments, built on the cited points and labeled as such in the text.
- **Helping a panicking person:** the Mental Health Foundation page gives little detail on helping others (stay, explain, reassure). The extra steps (low voice, one simple job, do not leave alone) are guide judgment; "shield from onlookers/media, don't probe" is from the Minnesota-hosted PFA file.
